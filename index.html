Extract HD photos & videos from Instagram & Snapchat via view-source:

Guide & Link Helper (PC / Mobile)

On PC / Laptop

  1. Open the Instagram / Snapchat link in your browser (logged in if private).
  2. Press Ctrl + U (or add view-source: before the URL).
  3. Press Ctrl + A, then Ctrl + C.
  4. Click Paste below, then click Extract Media.

On Mobile (Android)

  1. Paste the Instagram / Snapchat link above → tap Copy view-source:.
  2. Paste it into your browser's address bar (Chrome/Brave) and press Enter.
  3. Long-press the text → Select all → Copy.
  4. Return here → tap Paste → Extract Media.
